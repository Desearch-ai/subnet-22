What we accomplished

  • a back-testable forecasting search engine
  • a low latency and high signal websocket capable of leading information diffusion on Polymarket
  • an update driven forecasting subnet with stable macro targets
  • a model of the impact of prediction markets on equities

This all comes together.

The search engine first allows us to fully control the execution of the agents and to evaluate their capability over long intervals before putting them in production. Secondly, it speeds up the testing of agents on Numinous, since they can robustly evaluate different architectures before submitting. With it also disappears the vulnerability tied to miners hosting part of the execution outside the sandbox.

The search engine architecture.

The websocket gives an update driven subnet an efficient way to understand what changed in the informational landscape from day N to day N+1. In production it also defines the update frequency (of say a trader), when we run our forecasters, we update them when we believe a part of the news landscape changed.

When you update thousands of nodes in an impact model you can’t afford to run search node by node, you need a way to map a news item to an event efficiently and quickly. That’s where the web-socket will play a role. It is on one side of the trade off between fast and slow forecasting.

We spent a lot of time building a question generation system which is not in the subnet anymore but this is not wasted work. It’s actually precisely the piece needed for our subnet forecasters to shine in our product. Our graph’s biggest edge will be on events which are not on prediction markets: think the risks in the AI infra layer or biotech R&D milestones.

The graph based model is our first step in the direction of building a real-time world model leveraging the forecasters which has always been Numinous’s north star. We chose equities as the target because of the high interest we got from potential customers, but our vision is to bring the same model to insurance, governments and beyond.

Where we are going

  1. The subnet aggregates the agents into a world class forecasting agent for macro and geopolitics - we will show the unequivocal results.
  2. As inference keeps getting better, we will build datasets and signals which are valuable to traders and terminals, scaling what we already did with Ares.
  3. Our macro forecasting model’s goal is first to detect causality and understand relationships among events. We make this system 10x better with the best Numinous agent. We sell this to institutions, starting with commodities traders.
  4. Hyperliquid presents a unique opportunity by being at the intersection of prediction markets and equities. We believe that Hyperliquid’s share of the prediction market space will grow significantly. We want to position Numinous ahead of that by selling to traders both our inference and our models at the intersection of equities and prediction markets. We expect this to first take the form of a terminal.
  5. As our target landscape scales, we need to make inference cheaper. We thus distill our own forecasting model.
  6. As we move forward, grow the subnet, leverage the best miners and grow the team, we get increasingly close to a real-time world model which will help humanity make better decisions in all realms of life.
