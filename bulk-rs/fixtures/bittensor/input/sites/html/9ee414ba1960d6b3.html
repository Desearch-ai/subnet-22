// WHO WE ARE

qBitTensor Labs operates at the intersection of quantum computing, AI, and decentralized networks. We use Bittensor’s decentralized incentivization structure to advance deep tech — allowing anyone to participate.

From research labs to hacker lairs, we bring together the brightest and most creative minds. We believe breakthroughs happen when more perspectives and more unique expertise converge, not just traditional elites.

Quantum has hit a commercial tipping point — McKinsey counts 300+ companies already adopting it, and the credible near-term path runs through hybrid classical-quantum systems. That is exactly where open, incentivized competition pulls the frontier forward fastest.
