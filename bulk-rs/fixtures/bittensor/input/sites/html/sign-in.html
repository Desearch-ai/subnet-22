How it is made
1

Submit

Miners upload a ternary GGUF with the teacher's exact layout: every weight −1, 0 or +1 times a block scale, the file capped at Bonsai 2's size. The codes and scales are theirs to set.

2

Assay

The validator reads every submission against the unquantized teacher on the same prompts: the divergence of its next-token distribution from the teacher's, token by token.

3

Crown

A submission measurably closer than the reigning king becomes king, earns the subnet's emission, and is mirrored to Hugging Face with its 1.75-bit file. Watch the queue.
