01 /The idea

Your data.
Your time. Your budget.

DaniLabs stands for Distributed Accelerated Neural Intelligence. We’re developing an inference subnet on Bittensor: a network focused on running AI models.

Our priorities are no data retention, fast responses, and low cost. They guide how we’re developing the network and the experience of using it.

02 /Three priorities

Design priorities for the network we’re building.

No data retention.

We’re designing for prompts and responses to be processed without being stored after a request.

01 — Your data

Fast responses.

Optimize the time from a request to a useful response. Speed is a core priority for the inference network.

02 — Your time

Low-cost inference.

Bring down the cost of inference for developers and applications. Costs will take shape alongside the offering.

03 — Your budget
