**Desearch 2.0: building search together**

Search for AI needs relevant information without excessive waiting.

In 1.0, miners and validators helped us deliver useful search. But good answers did not always leave us with retrieval capabilities we could maintain, and response time remained a constraint.

With 2.0, we want SN22 contributions to build lasting search capabilities: permitted source data, searchable representations, and better models and ranking methods. Bittensor gives us a basis for recurring open participation and competition. We aim to make adopted model work openly reusable under appropriate licenses.

The first proposed work is crawling and refreshing useful pages, then computing embeddings with a selected model. That is execution work, not model invention. A bounded reranking experiment is an early candidate for testing new methods.

The team would initially operate the index and customer API. Miners would build and improve components in the background; validators would assess applicable subnet work. Reward assessment and customer deployment remain separate decisions.

This is a direction update—not the opening of a new reward program or an instruction to change software, hardware, weights or API integrations.

We will share written progress updates weekly, including what worked, what failed and what comes next.
