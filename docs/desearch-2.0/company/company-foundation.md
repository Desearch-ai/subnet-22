# Desearch — company foundation

**Updated:** 2026-09-06

## Purpose

AI applications need relevant information soon enough to use it. Search quality includes relevance, response time, freshness, coverage and dependable operation—not accuracy in isolation.

Desearch exists to improve that access. Its ambition is a search product whose ability to improve is not limited to the people it can hire.

## Founding belief

> **The future of search should not depend only on the team that operates it.**

Useful expertise exists across source specialists, infrastructure operators, researchers, model builders and evaluators. We want a practical route for that expertise to improve one product.

## Company identity

Desearch is a search infrastructure company. It develops and operates the product, serves customers and takes responsibility for securing the rights needed to integrate and use internal and external contributions.

The network broadens who can build and improve the product. It does not replace the need for an excellent team. The open network on Bittensor is part of that identity, not an interchangeable supplier.

## Mission

> **Build better search for AI through open competition.**

“Better” means useful search under real quality, latency, reliability and cost constraints. Open competition gives contributors a route to propose, test and improve methods, including by building on one another's work.

## Vision

> **A dependable search system, built and improved by our team and contributors around the world—from its data to its models and ranking—so AI can find relevant information fast.**

Accepted contributions should create capabilities the product can keep using and improving.

## Category

> **Search infrastructure for AI.**

## Company description

> **Desearch is building a search product whose index, models and ranking can be built and improved through an open network on Bittensor. Useful contributions are evaluated and integrated into one dependable Search API, designed to deliver relevant information fast.**

## Strategic shorthand

> **Build search together. Serve it as one.**

## Scope

Public web is our initial scope. Private/company knowledge is a possible later Enterprise extension requiring isolation and access controls.

The enduring company identity does not depend on a release number, a particular model, a storage topology or a reward formula.

## Initial customer focus

The selected workloads are:

| Workload | Intended output | Evaluation priority |
|---|---|---|
| Fast public-web retrieval for AI agents | Ranked sources and usable supporting text within a declared response-time budget | Measure retrieval-only performance first |
| Deeper research | More extensive source coverage and supporting material within a research-appropriate time budget | Evaluate separately from fast retrieval |

The switching failures selected for testing are missing relevant sources, stale information, weak or absent supporting text, and unacceptable waiting. These define the customer problems to investigate; they do not announce a new endpoint or establish an already-proven customer segment.
