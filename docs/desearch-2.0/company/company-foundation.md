# Desearch: company foundation

## What is Desearch?

Desearch is building a web index for AI through an open contribution network on Bittensor.

We collect and update web information and develop models that help AI find relevant sources. Search is the first application, with support for both fast retrieval and deeper research.

## Why we exist

AI applications need relevant sources, current information and supporting text they can use. Missing sources, stale pages and long waits can prevent them from completing a task well.

Meeting those needs takes continuing work: finding useful pages, extracting their content, keeping them current and improving how they are searched. Preparing information before a query arrives allows that work to serve many requests.

## Our mission

**Build a better web index for AI through open competition.**

Better means useful coverage, organized and current information, and relevant results delivered at a practical speed and cost. Competition should improve both the information in the index and the technology used to search it.

## Why an open network

We believe the people who can improve the web's usefulness for AI extend far beyond any one company.

Source specialists can bring missing coverage. Data operators can improve extraction and freshness. Researchers and model builders can improve retrieval and ranking. An open network gives people with different expertise a way to contribute and have their work evaluated under published rules.

Bittensor's SN22 provides the network of miners, validators and protocol incentives through which we intend to organize and reward contributions. Desearch remains responsible for choosing useful problems, integrating accepted work, securing the rights needed to use it and delivering a dependable product.

The [network rationale](open-network-and-bittensor.md) explains participation, evaluation and responsibilities in more detail.

## Who we serve and where we focus

Our initial customer focus is developers building AI agents and research applications. We are designing search around two workloads:

- **Fast search for AI agents.** Ranked sources and usable supporting text returned quickly enough for an agent to continue its task.
- **Deeper research.** A wider range of sources and more supporting text for complex questions that take longer to investigate.

Our initial source scope is the public web: company websites, news, articles, blogs, documentation and other useful pages. We may also include information from social platforms. We will begin with selected sources and search problems, then expand as we demonstrate useful results.

## Our vision

**A continuously improving web index, built with an open network, that AI can search, learn from and build on.**

We want useful information and better search methods to remain available for future requests and applications. The same collection could support structured datasets about companies, maintained information feeds and data for other AI systems or subnets.

Each future offering will need a clear customer need, suitable quality standards and the rights required for its intended use.
