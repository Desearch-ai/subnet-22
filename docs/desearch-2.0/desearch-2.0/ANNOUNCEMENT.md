**Desearch 2.0: a web index built through open competition**

In 1.0, miners and validators delivered useful search. But answers could take too long, and external dependencies limited what we could retain and improve.

2.0 builds an organized, refreshed collection of company websites, news, articles and other web information. Fast search comes first; the same data can later support specialized datasets and other AI systems. Preparing data before queries arrive reduces waiting and makes accepted work reusable.

Why Bittensor? To bring more contributors into building useful data and improving retrieval technology through open competition.

The first task families, crawling and embedding, run on SN22: miners fetch assigned pages and upload the extracted text, then turn that text into vectors (search representations) with Desearch's embedding model, and validators check a drawn share of both. The team operates the index and API. Valid work qualifies for rewards before customer deployment.

We plan to expand beyond these tasks. Possible later programs include model training, fine-tuning and reranking; their sequence and terms are not set.

Embedding miners need a GPU. Setup is in the [miner](../../miner.md) and [validator](../../validator.md) guides. No other program is open yet.
